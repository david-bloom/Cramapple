One-line: an MCQ answer row — the only place a rounded shape (the radio dot) exists in CramApple.

```jsx
<RadioOptionRow letter="B" selected verdict="distractor" showVerdict
  explanation="'will score' turns a prediction about averages into a guarantee about one student."
  note="Say 'predicted mean score', not 'will score'.">
  She will score 78 on the final.
</RadioOptionRow>
```

Variants: `eliminated` for ruled-out choices (clay, struck, 55%); `showVerdict` is on from the start in Open Hand and only after submission in Practice.

## Items

### Item 1 — MCQ (Easy · Calculate)

A net force of 10 N acts on a 2 kg object. What is its acceleration?

- A. 20 m/s²
- **B. 5 m/s²** ✔
- C. 0.2 m/s²
- D. 12 m/s²

*Key:* B. `a = F_net / m = 10 / 2 = 5 m/s²`.

### Item 2 — MCQ (Easy · Calculate)

An object is dropped from rest. Using `g = 10 m/s²`, what is its speed after
2.0 s (ignore air resistance)?

- A. 5 m/s
- B. 10 m/s
- **C. 20 m/s** ✔
- D. 40 m/s

*Key:* C. `v = v₀ + g·t = 0 + 10·2 = 20 m/s`.

### Item 3 — MCQ (Easy · Determine)

Which equation is Newton's second law?

- **A. `F_net = m·a`** ✔
- B. `F = m·v`
- C. `a = m·F`
- D. `F = ½·m·v²`

*Key:* A. (D is kinetic energy; B and C are not physical laws.)

### Item 4 — Short FRQ (Medium · Calculate, Justify)

A 3.0 kg box on a horizontal surface is pushed with a 15 N horizontal force. A
friction force of 3.0 N opposes the motion.

(a) Find the net horizontal force on the box.
(b) Find the box's acceleration.

*Expected response (development fixture, not a gold label):*
(a) `F_net = 15 − 3.0 = 12 N` in the direction of the push.
(b) `a = F_net / m = 12 / 3.0 = 4.0 m/s²`.

*Criterion sketch:* C1 — net force uses the vector sum (applied minus friction) =
12 N (guards FP-PHYS1-2.1-01 E6); C2 — correct `a = F_net/m = 4.0 m/s²`.

## Items

### Item 1 — MCQ (Easy · Calculate)

A 12 V battery is connected across a single 4 Ω resistor. What is the current?

- A. 48 A
- **B. 3 A** ✔
- C. 0.33 A
- D. 8 A

*Key:* B. `I = V/R = 12/4 = 3 A`.

### Item 2 — MCQ (Medium · Calculate)

How much power is dissipated by the 4 Ω resistor in Item 1?

- A. 4 W
- B. 12 W
- **C. 36 W** ✔
- D. 48 W

*Key:* C. `P = I·V = 3·12 = 36 W` (equivalently `V²/R = 144/4 = 36 W`).

### Item 3 — MCQ (Medium · Calculate)

Two 4 Ω resistors are connected in **parallel**. What is their equivalent
resistance?

- A. 8 Ω
- **B. 2 Ω** ✔
- C. 4 Ω
- D. 0.5 Ω

*Key:* B. `1/R = 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2 ⇒ R = 2 Ω`.
*Distractor logic:* A adds them as if in series (guards FP-PHYS2-4.1-01 E4).

### Item 4 — Short FRQ (Medium · Calculate)

Two 6 Ω resistors are connected in **series** across a 12 V battery.

(a) Find the total resistance and the current from the battery.
(b) Find the voltage across each resistor.

*Expected response (development fixture, not a gold label):*
(a) `R_total = 6 + 6 = 12 Ω`; `I = V/R = 12/12 = 1.0 A` (same current through both).
(b) Each resistor: `V = I·R = 1.0·6 = 6.0 V` (the two drops sum to the 12 V source).

*Criterion sketch:* C1 — series resistances added to 12 Ω and Ohm's law applied
for `I = 1.0 A` (guards FP E6); C2 — correct 6.0 V across each, consistent with the
same series current.

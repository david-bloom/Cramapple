## Items

### Item 1 — MCQ (Medium · Determine)

Two point charges exert a force of magnitude `F` on each other at separation `r`.
If the separation is increased to `2r`, the force becomes:

- A. `F/2`
- **B. `F/4`** ✔
- C. `2F`
- D. `F`

*Key:* B. Coulomb's law is inverse-square: `F ∝ 1/r²`, so doubling `r` gives
`F/4` (guards FP-PHYSCEM-1.1-01 E4).

### Item 2 — MCQ (Easy · Determine)

The magnitude of the electric field a distance `r` from a point charge `Q` is:

- A. `k·Q·r`
- **B. `k·Q / r²`** ✔
- C. `k·Q / r`
- D. `k·Q² / r`

*Key:* B.

### Item 3 — MCQ (Easy · Determine)

The electric field produced by an isolated **positive** point charge points:

- **A. radially away from the charge** ✔
- B. radially toward the charge
- C. in circles around the charge
- D. there is no field

*Key:* A.

### Item 4 — Short FRQ (Hard · Calculate, Justify)

A point charge `Q = +3.0 μC` sits in vacuum. Use `k = 9.0 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²`.

(a) Find the magnitude and direction of the electric field at a point 2.0 m away.
(b) Find the force on a `+1.0 μC` test charge placed at that point.

*Expected response (development fixture, not a gold label):*
(a) `E = k·Q/r² = (9.0×10⁹)(3.0×10⁻⁶)/(2.0)² = 2.7×10⁴ / 4 = 6.75×10³ N/C`,
directed radially **away** from `Q` (positive source).
(b) `F = q·E = (1.0×10⁻⁶)(6.75×10³) = 6.75×10⁻³ N`, also directed away from `Q`.

*Criterion sketch:* C1 — inverse-square field `k·Q/r²` computed to ≈ 6.75×10³ N/C
with outward direction (guards FP E6); C2 — `F = qE` applied for ≈ 6.75×10⁻³ N in
the correct direction (guards FP E5).

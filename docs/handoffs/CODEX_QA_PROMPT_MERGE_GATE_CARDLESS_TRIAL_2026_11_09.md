## Product and security checks
**A. Contract fidelity.** App vs `get_trial_state`, `start_subject_trial`, `get_my_learning_sessions`: field names, phases, typed errors (`trial:disabled|forbidden|subject_unavailable|already_used|missing_privacy_notice_version`, `not_authenticated`, `grading_access:trial_daily_cap`), idempotent replay and `replayed_other_subject`. The app must never count days or format trial end dates itself.
**B. Access control (the core risk).** With throwaway Development users (delete them and all rows afterwards; set the Development switch back to false): a trial row admits exactly one subject and nothing else (serving, `attempt-response`, `evaluate-attempt`, More help, `get_study_subject_keys`, `student-session-items`, `get_question_experience` for another subject), and denies everything after `ends_at` except own-work readback. Use `ends_at`, never `status` (`trial_v1` rows are `status='active'` with past `ends_at`). Try to get a second subject, a second trial (same email with different casing or `+` alias, second account), a subject change after confirming (D9), a longer `ends_at`, or another user's sessions through `get_my_learning_sessions`. Confirm cardless rows can never be charged or extended by `charge-due-trials`, `trial_claim_due` or the sweep, that a paid entitlement supersedes the trial, and that the sample supersede trigger is correct. Confirm the daily grading ceiling (40) and its denial.

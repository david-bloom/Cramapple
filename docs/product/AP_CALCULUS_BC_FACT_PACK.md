## Exam structure

- Hybrid digital exam with handwritten free-response work.
- 42 multiple-choice questions and 6 free-response questions; each section contributes 50% of the exam score.
- Multiple-choice: 29 no-calculator questions and 13 questions for which a graphing calculator is required for some items.
- Free response: 2 calculator-required problems and 4 no-calculator problems.
- Problems use analytical, graphical, tabular, and verbal representations and mix procedures, concepts, justification, and contextual modeling.

## Course practices for Cramapple tagging

1. Select and carry out appropriate mathematical procedures.
2. Connect analytical, graphical, numerical/tabular, and verbal representations.
3. Justify conclusions and solutions with mathematical reasoning.
4. Communicate with correct notation, units, definitions, and conventions.

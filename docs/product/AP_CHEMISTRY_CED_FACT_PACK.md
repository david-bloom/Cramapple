## 5. FRQ archetypes for content authoring

Based on the exam structure (3 long + 4 short), typical FRQ patterns include:

- **Calculation-heavy** (stoichiometry, equilibrium, thermodynamics, electrochemistry) — long or short
- **Explanation/argumentation** (particle-level reasoning, trend justification) — typically short
- **Lab/experimental design** (procedure, data analysis, error analysis) — typically long
- **Representation** (drawing Lewis structures, energy diagrams, particulate diagrams) — short
- **Mixed** (calculation + explanation in multi-part) — long

## 7. Grading-relevant subject characteristics

- Heavy reliance on **stoichiometric calculations** with unit conversions and significant figures.
- **Symbolic notation**: chemical formulas, balanced equations, electron configurations, Lewis structures.
- **Particulate-level reasoning**: explaining macroscopic observations via molecular/atomic behavior.
- **Graph/table interpretation**: rate law plots, titration curves, phase diagrams, Beer-Lambert data.
- **Equilibrium and acid-base calculations** (ICE tables, Henderson-Hasselbalch, Ksp).
- **Electrochemistry**: cell potential calculations, Faraday's law, Nernst equation.
- Calculator permitted — numeric answers expected to proper sig figs.

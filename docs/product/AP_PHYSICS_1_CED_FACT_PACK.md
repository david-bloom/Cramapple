## 5. FRQ archetypes for content authoring

The four revised FRQ types:
- **Mathematical Routines** — multi-step calculation, symbolic derivation, unit analysis
- **Translation Between Representations** — converting between diagrams, graphs, equations, verbal descriptions
- **Experimental Design and Analysis** — design procedures, analyze data, evaluate results, identify sources of error
- **Qualitative/Quantitative Translation** — connecting conceptual reasoning to mathematical predictions

## 7. Grading-relevant subject characteristics

- **Algebra-based** — no calculus; equations involve algebra, trig, and vector components.
- **Diagram-heavy**: free-body diagrams, motion diagrams, energy bar charts, circuit diagrams, graphs of position/velocity/acceleration vs time.
- **Vector reasoning**: sign conventions, component decomposition, direction matters as much as magnitude.
- **Unit analysis**: dimensional reasoning is a core skill; sig figs less emphasized than in Chemistry.
- **Conceptual explanations**: many FRQ parts require qualitative reasoning with physics principles, not just calculation.
- **Graph interpretation**: slopes, areas under curves, intercepts carry physical meaning.
- Calculator permitted — numeric answers expected with correct units.

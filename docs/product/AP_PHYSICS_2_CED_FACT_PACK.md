## 5. FRQ archetypes for content authoring

Same four types as Physics 1:
- **Mathematical Routines** — multi-step calculation, symbolic derivation
- **Translation Between Representations** — ray diagrams, circuit diagrams, field lines, graphs ↔ equations
- **Experimental Design and Analysis** — design procedures, analyze data, evaluate results
- **Qualitative/Quantitative Translation** — conceptual reasoning ↔ mathematical predictions

## 7. Grading-relevant subject characteristics

- **Algebra-based** — no calculus; same math level as Physics 1.
- **Diagram-heavy**: ray diagrams, circuit diagrams, field-line diagrams, PV diagrams, wave/interference patterns.
- **Broader conceptual scope**: thermodynamics, E&M, optics, waves, modern physics — more qualitative reasoning than Physics 1.
- **Quantum/modern physics**: wave-particle duality, photoelectric effect, nuclear decay — conceptual items, not heavy calculation.
- **Optics**: ray tracing, thin lens/mirror equations, diffraction/interference calculations.
- **Circuit analysis**: Kirchhoff's rules, RC circuits, power dissipation.
- Calculator permitted — numeric answers with correct units.

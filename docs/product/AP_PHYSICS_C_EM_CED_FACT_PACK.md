## 5. FRQ archetypes for content authoring

Same four types as Physics C: Mechanics, with **E&M-specific calculus**:
- **Mathematical Routines** — Gauss's law flux integrals, potential from field integration, Biot-Savart integrals, RC/LR/LC circuit differential equations
- **Translation Between Representations** — field-line diagrams, equipotential surfaces, circuit diagrams ↔ equations, B-field sketches
- **Experimental Design and Analysis** — design circuits, measure resistivity, verify Ohm's law, inductance measurements
- **Qualitative/Quantitative Translation** — physical reasoning about field behavior ↔ integral/differential expressions

## 7. Grading-relevant subject characteristics

- **Calculus-based E&M**: surface/line integrals (Gauss's law, Ampère's law, Faraday's law), differential equations (RC charging/discharging: q(t) = Q₀(1-e^(-t/RC)), LR circuits: I(t) = (ε/R)(1-e^(-tR/L)), LC oscillations).
- **Symbolic derivation**: many FRQ parts require deriving field/potential/current expressions from first principles.
- **Vector fields**: E and B fields have direction and magnitude; cross products (F = qv × B), dot products (Φ = ∫E·dA).
- **Circuit analysis**: Kirchhoff's rules with multi-loop circuits, transient behavior of RC/LR/LC circuits.
- **Overlap with Physics 2**: Units 10–11 (circuits) and Unit 12 (magnetism) cover similar topics at calculus depth. Items must require calculus, not algebra-level solutions.
- **Diagram-heavy**: field-line diagrams, equipotential maps, circuit schematics, Gaussian surfaces, Amperian loops.
- Calculator permitted — but many answers are symbolic.

## Exam structure

- Hybrid digital exam with handwritten free-response work.
- 42 multiple-choice questions, approximately 63% of the exam score.
- 4 free-response questions, approximately 37% of the exam score; each problem is worth 6 points.
- Multiple-choice: 29 no-calculator questions and 13 questions for which a graphing calculator is required for some items.
- Free response: 2 calculator-required and 2 no-calculator questions.
- The four public free-response focuses are function concepts, nonperiodic modeling, periodic modeling, and symbolic manipulation.

## Course practices for Cramapple tagging

1. Algebraically manipulate functions, equations, and expressions.
2. Translate among analytical, graphical, numerical/tabular, and verbal representations.
3. Communicate conclusions precisely and support them with mathematical rationales.

## Assessed units and public multiple-choice weight ranges

| Unit | Cramapple label | Public exam weight |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Polynomial and Rational Functions | 30%–40% |
| 2 | Exponential and Logarithmic Functions | 25%–40% |
| 3 | Trigonometric and Polar Functions | 30%–35% |

Unit 4, Functions Involving Parameters, Vectors, and Matrices, is part of the course but is not assessed on the current AP Exam. It is excluded from this exam-focused seed bank.

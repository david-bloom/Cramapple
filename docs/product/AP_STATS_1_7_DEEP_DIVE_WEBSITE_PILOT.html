Deep Dive

Match the statistic to the job

Summary statistics describe center, spread, position, and unusual values. Choose one that matches the question.

Mean and standard deviation are sensitive to extremes. Median and IQR resist them and often fit skewed data better.

New in 1.7

x̄ = (1/n) ∑xi
Mean: center using every observed value.
IQR = Q3 - Q1
IQR: resistant spread of the middle 50%.

Answer move

Turn a calculated value into a complete statistical answer

1

Name the job

Decide whether the prompt asks about center, spread, position, outliers, or resistance.

2

Choose and report

Name the statistic, show or state its value, and include units when they are available.

3

Justify the fit

Explain why the statistic suits the distribution, especially when skew or outliers affect resistance.

Review gate

Documented misconception

Do not report a number without naming what it summarizes. Do not default to a nonresistant statistic when the prompt emphasizes skew or outliers.

Existing production example · source-grounded

Worked example

Choose a typical value when one home price is extreme

A dataset of home prices contains one extremely expensive house. Which measure of center should a student report to describe a typical home price?

Weak response

Ignores resistance

“Use the mean because it uses all the numbers.”

Stronger response

Matches statistic to distribution

Use the median because it is resistant to the extremely expensive house. The mean would be pulled upward by that outlier.

Moderate-work rewrite

Why the stronger response works

It names the median, links resistance to the outlier, and explains why the mean would be misleading.

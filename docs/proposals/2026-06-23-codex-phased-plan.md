# Cramapple SEO and AEO Phased Plan

**Status:** Codex implementation plan  
**Date:** 2026-06-23

## Goal

Build Cramapple's marketing authority in a controlled sequence that supports
conversion without overbuilding content too early.

## Phase 1 - AP Biology Foundation

Deliver the first complete subject stack for AP Biology.

### Assets

- AP Biology pillar page.
- FRQ tips page.
- Unit 3 page: Cellular Energetics.
- Unit 6 page: Gene Expression and Regulation.
- Unit 7 page: Natural Selection.

### Why these pages first

- They map to the highest-value AP Biology content.
- They capture high-intent search and answer-engine queries.
- They give Cramapple one coherent subject cluster that can be linked from the
  homepage and future conversion pages.

### Phase 1 acceptance criteria

- The pillar page answers the core AP Biology questions directly.
- The FRQ page explains task words, scoring behavior, and common misses.
- The three unit pages cover the highest-priority units with practical student
  guidance.
- The page set uses the same score-maximization voice as the marketing site.

## Phase 2 - Subject Expansion

Replicate the AP Biology pattern for the next best subjects.

### Candidates

- AP Statistics
- AP Chemistry
- AP US History
- AP Calculus BC

### Objective

Create enough structured depth to establish topical authority without diluting
the conversion focus.

## Phase 3 - Tools and Authority

Add content and utilities that increase citation probability and bookmark value.

### Candidates

- score calculation / estimate content
- difficulty and comparison guides
- FAQ-rich pages for AI citation
- structured tables and answer capsules

## Phase 4 - Scale by Data

Expand based on search console data, conversion behavior, and the pages that
actually earn attention.

## Guardrails

- Do not turn this into a content factory too early.
- Do not let SEO content dilute the homepage conversion story.
- Keep every page tied back to student score value.

# AP Chemistry and AP Physics 1 Grading Test Matrix

**Status:** Draft calibration matrix
**Purpose:** Define the first grading-test coverage areas Codex and Claude should
use when validating the new subjects.

## Shared Rules

- Keep the packets governed and original.
- Preserve subject-specific notation and representation requirements.
- Include boundary cases, not just clean winners.
- Use gold-set calibration before any launch-readiness claim.
- Treat the test matrix as a planning artifact until the actual packets are
  assembled.

## AP Chemistry

### Primary test surfaces

- stoichiometry and dimensional analysis;
- balanced equations and notation;
- equilibrium and acid/base calculations;
- kinetics and thermodynamics reasoning;
- lab data interpretation;
- unit handling and significant figures; and
- explanation items with partial-credit boundaries.

### Test emphasis

- exact numeric checking;
- equation/notation validation;
- answer units and sig-fig behavior;
- misconception traps for common chemistry errors; and
- mixed representation items with text + table + equation.

## AP Physics 1

### Primary test surfaces

- vectors and sign conventions;
- kinematics and dynamics;
- energy and momentum;
- rotational or angular reasoning where applicable;
- graphs, slopes, and trends;
- circuits and conservation reasoning; and
- diagram-based explanation items.

### Test emphasis

- exact numeric checking;
- vector and sign-convention validation;
- graph interpretation;
- diagram interpretation;
- misconception traps for common physics errors; and
- mixed representation items with text + equation + diagram.

## Suggested Packet Shape

The first packet for each subject should be small enough to review quickly but
broad enough to exercise the new verifier rules.

Recommended packet components:

- MCQ;
- short FRQ;
- long FRQ where appropriate;
- at least one boundary item;
- at least one calculation-heavy item; and
- at least one explanation-heavy item.

## Readiness Check

The grading test work is ready to hand off when:

- the packet shape is agreed;
- the subject taxonomy is stable enough to target;
- the verification profile is written down;
- the calibration set exists; and
- the result is recorded in the launch-readiness packet.

## Pacing

45-60 minutes budgeted per learning target (LT1.1-1.8), 7-10 hours total for
half of Unit 1. Real checkpoint: a graded assessment on "day 3" of the school
year covering this material. Useful calibration point if Cramapple ever sizes
a Unit 1 diagnostic or onboarding module - a motivated student treats "half
of Unit 1" as roughly a school day's worth of focused work, not a five-minute
warm-up.

## Self-assessment framing

A 0-4 rubric scale per learning target: "I did not attempt" / "I tried, but I
don't understand yet" / "I'm starting to understand, but I still need
support" / "I understand the skill and can do it with a few mistakes" / "I
fully understand the skill and can do it correctly on my own." This is a
plain-language confidence scale distinct from a numeric score - worth
considering as a pre/post-practice self-check UX pattern, separate from our
actual grading.

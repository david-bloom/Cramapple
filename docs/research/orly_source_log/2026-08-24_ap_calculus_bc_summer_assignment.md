# Insight note: AP Calculus BC summer assignment (Solebury, Pritchett)

Source: `AP Calculus BC.pdf`. Logged in `SOURCE_LOG.md`.

## Category note (new pattern, not seen in the Calc AB / Chemistry batch)

This document is not teacher-authored worksheets. It's a DeltaMath assignment
(covering Units 1 & 2) plus three DeltaMath "Corrective Assignment" PDF
exports (Mid-Unit 1, End-of-Unit 1, Unit 2 CA) — DeltaMath's own
auto-generated remediation packets, built from whichever problems a specific
student got wrong. DeltaMath is licensed curriculum software, not the
school's original authorship, which makes this source *more* rights-sensitive
than a teacher-made packet, not less. Proposed protocol §2 addition below.

## Pacing — BC moves roughly 4x faster than AB over the summer

Solebury's AB summer assignment: 7-10 hours for half of Unit 1 (topics
1.1-1.8). Solebury's BC summer assignment: 5-8 hours for *all* of Unit 1
(1.1-1.16) *and* well into Unit 2 (2.1 through the quotient rule, 2.9) before
day one. Both courses assess with an early-year test, but BC's is "during the
first two weeks" per unit rather than a single day-3 quiz. This is a strong,
concrete pacing-ratio signal if Cramapple ever wants to calibrate a BC-track
onboarding module against the AB-track one rather than just scaling up
generically.

## Stakes signal

"Failure to complete the assignment before the deadline may result in removal
from the course." Meaningfully higher stakes framing than the AB document,
which only warned about falling behind. Consistent with BC being the
accelerated/self-selected track — worth keeping in mind if Cramapple ever
differentiates onboarding tone or urgency messaging by course level.

## Corrective-assignment structure is a validation signal, not just an insight

The "Corrective Assignment" packets are themselves close to what Cramapple's
own adaptive practice already aims to do: generate follow-up problems
targeted at exactly what a student got wrong, rather than a generic re-quiz.
Worth noting as external validation of the adaptive-practice direction, not
a new idea to copy from DeltaMath's specific implementation.

## Unit coverage this unlocks

Unlike the AB batch (Unit 1 only, first half), this document's Unit 2 content
gives us a first real external reference point for Unit 2 (Differentiation:
Definition and Fundamental Properties, 2.1-2.10) — average/instantaneous rate
of change, the limit definition of the derivative, estimating derivatives
from tables, derivative rules through the quotient rule, tangent lines, and
differentiability-from-piecewise-functions. All topic codes confirmed against
`app.taxonomy_topics` for `ap_calculus_bc`.

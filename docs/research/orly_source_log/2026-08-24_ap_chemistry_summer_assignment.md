# Insight note: AP Chemistry summer assignment (Solebury, Gavin)

Source: `AP Chemistry Summer Assignment.pdf`. Logged in `SOURCE_LOG.md`.

## Category gap this document exposes

Every topic in this packet - significant figures, scientific notation, metric
conversion, density, subatomic particles/isotopes, moles and molar mass,
polyatomic ions, nomenclature - sits *before* AP Chemistry's actual CED Unit 1
(Atomic Structure and Properties). None of it maps cleanly onto an existing
`taxonomy_topics` row, and forcing it into one would misrepresent what's being
tested.

This confirms Cramapple's taxonomy currently has no explicit "readiness" or
"Unit 0" layer distinct from CED units. That's a real product question, not
something to resolve by tagging: does Cramapple want a pre-Unit-1 diagnostic
category at all, and if so, does it apply per-subject or as a shared
cross-subject skill layer (sig figs and unit conversion recur well beyond
Chemistry)?

## Pacing / structure

No per-topic time budget given (unlike the Calc AB document from the same
batch) - this packet reads as a one-shot "read and answer" assignment rather
than a paced multi-lesson sequence. Weaker signal for calibrating our own
content pacing than the Calc AB document.

## CALC-H2  (hard)

**Problem (for context only — do not solve independently):** Find g'(x) for g(x) = x^2 * e^(-3x) using the product rule. Show your work.

**Copy this exactly, by hand:**

> g'(x) = (d/dx[x^2]) e^(-3x) + x^2 (d/dx[e^(-3x)])
> g'(x) = 2x e^(-3x) + x^2 (-3) e^(-3x)
> g'(x) = e^(-3x) (2x - 3x^2)

## CALC-H4  (hard)

**Problem (for context only — do not solve independently):** An inverted conical tank has radius 3 m and height 6 m at the top. Water drains at dV/dt = -2 m^3/min. Using similar triangles (r = h/2), find dh/dt when h = 4 m.

**Copy this exactly, by hand:**

> r = h/2  (similar triangles, R=3, H=6)
> V = (1/3) pi r^2 h = (1/3) pi (h/2)^2 h = (pi h^3) / 12
> dV/dt = (pi h^2 / 4) (dh/dt)
> -2 = (pi (4)^2 / 4) (dh/dt) = 4 pi (dh/dt)
> dh/dt = -1/(2 pi) m/min

## CALC-H5  (hard)

**Problem (for context only — do not solve independently):** Find the interval of convergence of the series: the sum from n=1 to infinity of (x-2)^n / (n * 3^n).

**Copy this exactly, by hand:**

> ratio test: limit of |a_(n+1)/a_n| = |x-2| / 3
> converges when |x-2| < 3, i.e. -1 < x < 5
> at x = -1: sum of (-1)^n / n converges (alternating harmonic)
> at x = 5: sum of 1/n diverges (harmonic series)
> interval of convergence: [-1, 5)

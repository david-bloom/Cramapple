## What to know

**There is no standalone "Gravitation" unit.** This course has never had one recently, but if you see any `apphycm-*` item labeled or framed around a standalone Gravitation topic, that framing is wrong — gravitational/orbital content lives under **Unit 6, Topic 6.6 ("Motion of Orbiting Satellites")**. Unit 2, Topic 2.6 ("Gravitational Force") covers gravity only as a force type (for use in dynamics problems), not orbital mechanics.

Rotation is split across two units, not one:
- Unit 5 (Torque and Rotational Dynamics): rotational kinematics, torque, Newton's laws in rotational form.
- Unit 6 (Energy and Momentum of Rotating Systems): rotational KE, angular momentum, rolling, and orbital motion (6.6).

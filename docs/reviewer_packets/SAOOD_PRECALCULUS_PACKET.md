## What to know before reviewing

- **Precalculus, not calculus.** No derivative/integral notation or limit-based reasoning — this course is about function behavior, rates of change described numerically/graphically, and modeling.
- **Unit 4 (Functions Involving Parameters, Vectors, and Matrices) is explicitly NOT assessed on the AP Exam.** If any item tests parametric functions, vectors, or matrices as if it were exam-scored content, flag it — that's a scope violation, not a content error, same category of issue we found and fixed in AP Statistics and AP Physics this cycle.
- **Per-question calculator policy is split, not uniform.** FRQ Question 1 (Function Concepts) and Question 2 (Modeling a Non-Periodic Context) require a graphing calculator; Question 3 (Modeling a Periodic Context) and Question 4 (Symbolic Manipulations) do not allow one. If you're reviewing an FRQ, check it respects the right policy for its question type.
- Heavy cross-unit theme: "rate of change" shows up in Units 1, 2, and 3 alike (not just Unit 1) — described numerically/graphically, not via calculus.

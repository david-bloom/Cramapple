# AP Biology 2026: Complete Review Guide, Exam Format & Practice

AP Biology is a college-level course equivalent to two semesters of introductory biology. The 2026 exam covers 8 units, combines digital multiple choice with handwritten free response, and rewards students who can think biologically under time pressure. Memorization helps, but it is not enough to earn the top scores.

This guide covers the exam format, unit weights, score distribution, major difficulty points, study planning, FRQ strategy, and where the biggest score opportunities live.

---

## What Is the AP Biology Exam? (Quick Answer)

The AP Biology exam is 3 hours long. It is split evenly between a multiple-choice section and a free-response section:

- 60 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes
- 6 free-response questions in 90 minutes
- 50% of the score from each section

The exam tests 8 units and emphasizes data analysis, experimental design, and applying biological concepts to new scenarios. Students who only memorize definitions usually lose points on the FRQ section.

**2026 exam date:** Monday, May 4, 2026, at 8:00 AM local time.

## Exam Format

| Section | Questions | Time | Score Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multiple Choice | 60 questions | 90 minutes | 50% |
| Free Response | 6 questions (2 long, 4 short) | 90 minutes | 50% |
| Total | 66 questions | 3 hours | 100% |

### Section I - Multiple Choice

Many questions are stimulus-based. They show a graph, table, diagram, or experimental setup and ask you to interpret it. The section is digital.

### Section II - Free Response

The FRQ section is handwritten in a paper booklet. The two long FRQs are worth 9 points each. The four short FRQs are worth 4 points each.

Calculator access and the formula sheet are provided. Students should still practice using the formulas before exam day.

### Hybrid Format

AP Biology uses a hybrid digital format. Students complete multiple choice in Bluebook and write free response by hand in a paper booklet. That makes handwritten response practice especially important.

## The 8 Units: What Is on the Exam

| Unit | Name | Exam Weight | What It Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chemistry of Life | 8-11% | Water, macromolecules, enzyme structure and function |
| 2 | Cell Structure and Function | 10-13% | Membranes, transport, compartments, cell-environment interactions |
| 3 | Cellular Energetics | 12-16% | Photosynthesis, cellular respiration, fermentation |
| 4 | Cell Communication and Cell Cycle | 10-15% | Signal transduction, feedback, mitosis, meiosis |
| 5 | Heredity | 8-11% | Mendelian and non-Mendelian inheritance, pedigrees |
| 6 | Gene Expression and Regulation | 12-16% | Transcription, translation, regulation, mutations, biotechnology |
| 7 | Natural Selection | 13-20% | Evolution, Hardy-Weinberg, phylogenetics, speciation |
| 8 | Ecology | 10-15% | Energy flow, population dynamics, cycles, communities |

The three heaviest units - Units 7, 6, and 3 - make up the largest share of the exam. If time is short, start there.

## Is AP Biology Hard?

AP Biology is challenging because it tests application, not just recall.

What makes it hard:

- FRQs require precise written reasoning.
- Many MCQs are stimulus-based.
- Unit 3 requires understanding pathways and energy flow.
- Unit 7 includes Hardy-Weinberg and population reasoning.

What makes it manageable:

- The formulas are provided.
- The pass rate is solid.
- Most score losses come from FRQ precision, not impossible content.

## FRQ Overview: What You Actually Need to Know

The FRQ section is where the biggest point gains live.

Common task words:

| Task Word | What It Demands |
|---|---|
| Describe | State observable features |
| Explain | Give a mechanism |
| Justify | Make a claim and support it |
| Predict | State an outcome based on a condition |
| Design | Outline an experiment with variables and controls |

The average student leaves a lot of points on the table because the answer is vague, even when the biology is correct.

## FAQ

### How hard is AP Biology?
It is a rigorous course, but the score distribution shows that many students pass when they prepare for the FRQ format and the data-based questions.

### How many questions are on the exam?
60 multiple-choice and 6 free-response questions.

### What is a good AP Biology score?
A 3 is passing. A 4 or 5 is stronger for college credit and program requirements.

### When are AP Biology scores released?
Usually in early July.

### Is a calculator allowed?
Yes, and the formula sheet is provided.

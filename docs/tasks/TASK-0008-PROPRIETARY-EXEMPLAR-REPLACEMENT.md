## Product Goal

Create a rights-safe, independently authored AP Biology MCQ package that can
serve as the first candidate proprietary exemplar without relying on, adapting,
or reconstructing any official question.

## Technical Scope

- Issue a blank versioned authoring brief for an AP Biology quantitative or
  data-analysis MCQ.
- Assign the brief to a paid qualified tutor who has not received the rejected
  candidate or its source description.
- Require a complete MCQ, rubric, teaching, transfer, delayed-retrieval,
  accessibility, source, rights, and originality package.
- Run calculation, completeness, distractor-distinctness, scientific,
  similarity, teaching, grading, accessibility, and rights checks.
- Determine whether the approved package is suitable as an authoring exemplar
  or only as production practice content.

## Out of Scope

- Rewriting or repairing the rejected official-derived candidate.
- Giving the author an official question, rejected question, scenario,
  organisms, values, distractors, or source locator.
- Treating one exemplar as sufficient validation for an authoring system.
- Production use before all gates pass.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Blank coverage brief approved.
- [ ] Author signs the required rights, confidentiality, and originality terms.
- [ ] Author attests that no official, restricted, rejected, or unauthorized
  question material was used.
- [ ] Package is independently solvable and internally complete.
- [ ] Every distractor maps to a distinct plausible error.
- [ ] All consequential scientific and quantitative claims are verified.
- [ ] Similarity and rights preflight pass.
- [ ] Two independent Teaching Validators approve.
- [ ] Applicable grading and accessibility gates pass.
- [ ] Product Owner records whether the package may enter the exemplar pool.

## QA Plan

- Verify every required input appears in the stimulus.
- Independently recompute the keyed answer.
- Review distractor uniqueness and diagnostic usefulness.
- Confirm no reviewer or author received the rejected artifact.
- Confirm the artifact remains immutable through review and revision.

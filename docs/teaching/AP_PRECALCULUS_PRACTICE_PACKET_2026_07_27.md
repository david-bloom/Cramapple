## Free-response questions

### FRQ 1 — Function Concepts (calculator)

A function f is modeled by f(x)=0.5x³−2x²−x+6 on −2≤x≤5.

(a) Find the average rate of change of f on [1,4].

(b) Use technology to approximate all real zeros of f in the interval to three decimals.

(c) Determine the intervals in the stated domain where f(x)>0, and justify from the zeros and a sign analysis.

### FRQ 2 — Function Concepts (calculator)

Let g(x)=3·1.4^x−5.

(a) Find g(2.5) to three decimals and state whether it is above or below the horizontal asymptote.

(b) Solve g(x)=20 to three decimals.

(c) Write g⁻¹(x) and state its domain.

### FRQ 3 — Function Concepts (calculator)

A rational function is h(x)=(x²−x−6)/(x²−4x+3).

(a) Identify every x-intercept, vertical asymptote, and hole.

(b) Find the horizontal asymptote and explain using end behavior.

(c) Solve h(x)=2, excluding values outside the domain.

### FRQ 4 — Function Concepts (calculator)

A function p has values p(0)=2, p(1)=5, p(2)=13, p(3)=35, and p(4)=97.

(a) Compute successive ratios and explain why a simple exponential model is not exact.

(b) Find constants a and b so p(x)=a·2^x+b·3^x matches p(0) and p(1).

(c) Use the model to predict p(5), and compare it with the pattern in the table.

### FRQ 5 — Function Concepts (calculator)

For x>0, let q(x)=ln(x)+2/x.

(a) Find q(1) and q(e²) exactly.

(b) Use technology to approximate the absolute minimum value of q and an x-value where it occurs on 0.2≤x≤8.

(c) Explain the end behavior of q as x approaches 0 from the right and as x increases without bound.

### FRQ 6 — Modeling Nonperiodic (calculator)

A ball’s height y, in meters, is modeled as a quadratic function of time t, in seconds. Measurements give (t,y)=(0,2),(2,10),(5,7).

(a) Construct the quadratic model y=at²+bt+c by giving a system and solving for a, b, and c.

(b) Find the average rate of change in height from t=1 to t=4, with units.

(c) A student uses the model at t=20. Explain one limitation of that prediction.

### FRQ 7 — Modeling Nonperiodic (calculator)

A medication sample contains 160 milligrams at t=0 hours and 80 milligrams at t=4 hours. Assume an exponential model M(t)=ab^t.

(a) Use the two measurements to determine a and b and write the model.

(b) Find the average rate of change from t=2 to t=6, with units.

(c) Explain why the model should not be used to claim the sample reaches exactly 0 milligrams at a finite time.

### FRQ 8 — Modeling Nonperiodic (calculator)

Daily profit P, in hundreds of dollars, is quadratic in production x, measured in hundreds of units. Profit is zero at x=20 and x=80, and P(50)=90.

(a) Construct a quadratic model for P by determining its leading coefficient.

(b) Find the average rate of change of profit from x=30 to x=60, with contextual units.

(c) Explain why the model should not automatically be extrapolated to x=200.

### FRQ 9 — Modeling Nonperiodic (calculator)

A tree is 1.8 meters tall when planted and approaches a limiting height of 9.3 meters. After 5 years it is 6.803 meters tall. Use H(t)=L−Ab^t.

(a) Use the information to determine L, A, and b to three decimals and write the model.

(b) Find the average rate of change from t=0 to t=5, with units.

(c) Explain one reason the model may not accurately predict the height of every individual tree after 40 years.

### FRQ 10 — Modeling Nonperiodic (calculator)

Water temperature T, in degrees Celsius, is modeled logarithmically by depth x in meters. Measurements are T(1)=18 and T(4)=24. Use T(x)=a+b ln x.

(a) Use the measurements to determine a and b and write the model.

(b) Find the average rate of change in temperature from x=1 to x=4, with units.

(c) Explain why the model cannot be used for x≤0 and why very large-depth extrapolation may be unreasonable.

### FRQ 11 — Modeling Periodic (no calculator)

(a) Give five consecutive key points for one cycle beginning at t=0 and state the midline.

(c) On 0<t<3, state whether d is increasing or decreasing and whether its rate of change is increasing or decreasing.

### FRQ 12 — Modeling Periodic (no calculator)

### FRQ 13 — Modeling Periodic (no calculator)

### FRQ 15 — Modeling Periodic (no calculator)

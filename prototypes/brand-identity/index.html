Visual Identity — Proposal v0.1

Maximize your time. Maximize your score.

Built like a tech product, not a study app — the confident minimalism of Apple or Chrome, not the classroom warmth of a tutoring brand. Earns trust through precision, not encouragement.

Geometric, abstract apple echo. No bite notch — reads as its own mark, not Apple Inc.-adjacent. Single flat color, simple enough to hold up at favicon size.
01 — Logo / Wordmark

Wordmark-led. The mark is a quiet accent.

This is a desktop web product, not a mobile app where an icon glyph has to carry the brand alone. The full name in the chosen typography is the primary identity surface — the mark appears small, sparingly, mostly as a tab icon or alongside the wordmark.

04 — Voice

Confident, economical, precise.

Closer to Apple's terse certainty than an encouraging-teacher tone. Honest about gaps, never hyped — and doesn't lead with "AI" as the sell.

  • No AI-as-headline. Pedagogy and rigor lead; the model is a quiet implementation detail.
  • Credibility, not novelty. The family story is evidence of rigor (primary sources on the exact exam), not a founder-story charm device.
  • Feels like a great tutor, looks like Apple. Interaction tone and visual brand temperature are different axes — warmth in the feedback, restraint in the chrome.
  • Reframes urgency as efficiency. No countdown timers, no FOMO banners, no manufactured panic.
05 — Motion

Fast, purposeful, no bounce.

Motion communicates a state change, not delight. No spring/overshoot easing, no celebratory animation, no gamified reward motion (no confetti, no streak pop, no mascot dance).

  • 120–260ms — short enough to feel immediate, never sluggish.
  • Ease-out only — quick start, gentle settle. No bounce, no spring, no overshoot.
  • Small displacement — a few pixels of movement, not slides or zooms.
  • No idle motion — nothing animates unless the user or system state actually changed.

A plant cell is placed in a solution with lower water potential than the cell's contents. Describe how water moves across the plasma membrane and explain how turgor pressure changes as a result.

Prototype-only original placeholder. Not approved Cramapple content.

Question decision Approve or propose change

Use this field to propose a revision to the FRQ prompt, context, or expected response boundaries.

Canonical answer Pending review

Water moves from the cell (higher water potential) to the surrounding solution (lower water potential) by osmosis through aquaporin channels in the plasma membrane. As water leaves, the cell shrinks away from the cell wall (plasmolysis) and turgor pressure decreases to zero or below zero.

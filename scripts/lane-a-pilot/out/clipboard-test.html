Copy-notes clipboard test

Puts the real Copy-notes payload on your clipboard — same two flavours (text/html + text/plain) and the same ClipboardItem call production would use. Paste each one into a Google Doc.

What to look for

  1. A pastes with both diagrams visible → inline SVG is fine, we delete the warning.
  2. A loses the diagrams but B keeps them → rasterise at copy time; no hosting needed.
  3. Both A and B lose them → the warning stays and public raster URLs become the real fix.
  4. In every case the written description and labelled parts should survive — that is C.

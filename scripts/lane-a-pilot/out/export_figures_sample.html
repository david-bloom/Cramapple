Approach to equilibrium

Concentrations change, then level off (figure 1 of 2)

What the figure shows. Concentration against time for a reactant and a product. The reactant curve falls and the product curve rises, and both flatten to constant values after which there is no net change. Illustrative data.

Labelled parts.

  • Concentration or partial pressure vs. time
  • Constant region

Illustrative values.

Flat lines mean no net change, not that the reaction has stopped.

Approach to equilibrium: rates

Forward and reverse rates converge and become equal (figure 2 of 2)

What the figure shows. Forward and reverse reaction rates against time. The forward rate falls and the reverse rate rises until the two meet at a common value and stay there. Equal rates, not zero rates, is what equilibrium means. Illustrative data.

Labelled parts.

  • Rate vs. time
  • Constant region

Illustrative values.

Equal rates, not zero rates: both processes continue.

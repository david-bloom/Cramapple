Approach to equilibrium
Concentrations change, then level off (figure 1 of 2)
Concentration against time for a reactant and a product. The reactant curve falls and the product curve rises, and both flatten to constant values after which there is no net change. Illustrative data.
  - Concentration or partial pressure vs. time
  - Constant region
Illustrative values.

Approach to equilibrium: rates
Forward and reverse rates converge and become equal (figure 2 of 2)
Forward and reverse reaction rates against time. The forward rate falls and the reverse rate rises until the two meet at a common value and stay there. Equal rates, not zero rates, is what equilibrium means. Illustrative data.
  - Rate vs. time
  - Constant region
Illustrative values.
